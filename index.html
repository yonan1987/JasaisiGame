About the Photographer

The Art of
Seeing Light

For over fifteen years, I've been chasing moments where light and shadow converge to reveal something unseen. Based in New York and working across continents, my practice spans portrait, landscape, street, and commercial photography — unified by an obsession with the poetic weight of a single frame.

My work has appeared in Vogue, The New York Times, National Geographic, and has been exhibited at the International Center of Photography and Galerie Perrotin. I believe every photograph is a conversation between what is present and what is absent.

Whether it's a quiet portrait or a sweeping landscape, I seek the silence within the noise — the moment that makes you pause.
